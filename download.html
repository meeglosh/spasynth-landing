Installing

  1. Quit your music software, then open the installer.
  2. Get the sound library with SPAStation, our free download app. It installs the starter library straight into your SPASynth library folder. If you already own our sound libraries, point SPASynth at them with SET LIBRARY.
  3. Bought SPASynth? Your serial is in SPAStation, in the Licenses view. Click the TRIAL badge in SPASynth to activate.

A note for Windows

Our installers are digitally signed by Kenzora Games, Inc. Windows SmartScreen may still show a blue "Windows protected your PC" message for a new installer. Check that the publisher reads Kenzora Games, Inc., then click "More info" and "Run anyway".
